# Reference exchange rates — March 2026

Monthly average reference rates published for accounting use. Rates are expressed as
units of foreign currency per one euro.

| Currency | Code | Average, March 2026 | End of month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pound sterling | GBP | 0.855 | 0.851 |
| US dollar | USD | 1.082 | 1.079 |
| Swiss franc | CHF | 0.951 | 0.948 |
| Polish zloty | PLN | 4.291 | 4.284 |
| Danish krone | DKK | 7.459 | 7.459 |

Notes. These are reference rates for reporting, not dealing rates. A transaction
settled through a payment provider will differ by that provider's spread, which for
card acquiring in this corridor is typically 1.5 to 2.5 percent. Accounting policy at
most retailers is to translate at the monthly average and to book the spread as a
separate cost line rather than to distort the revenue figure.

Published 1 April 2026, covering the month of March 2026.

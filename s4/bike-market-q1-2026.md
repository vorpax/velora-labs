# European bicycle market, Q1 2026

The first quarter opened weakly across most western European markets, with unit volumes
down modestly against a strong comparative period. Cargo formats continued to outgrow
the market as a whole.

Currency movement was a live factor for operators selling into the United Kingdom. The
euro strengthened against sterling through the quarter, which compresses the euro value
of a sterling-denominated sale unless the retail price is repriced.

Discounting was widespread in March as retailers cleared model-year stock ahead of the
spring season. Observed discounting clustered between 10 and 18 percent off recommended
retail, above the levels seen in the same period a year earlier.

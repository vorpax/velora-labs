# Velora commercial pricing notes — model year 2026

Recommended retail prices, including VAT, for the EU market.

| Range | Model | RRP incl. VAT | Standard trade cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trail X | mountain, full suspension | EUR 3 290 | EUR 1 490 |
| Urban S | city, hub drive | EUR 1 690 | EUR 742 |
| Cargo Pro | long-tail cargo | EUR 2 690 | EUR 1 358 |
| Velora Fold | folding commuter | EUR 1 390 | EUR 618 |
| Aero | helmet | EUR 129 | EUR 41 |

Discounting. Field discounting is permitted to a floor of 12 percent off RRP without
approval. Below that floor an order requires commercial sign-off, and the standard
trade cost above is the figure against which the margin is assessed.

Trade cost is ex-works and excludes inbound freight, duty and the inbound quality
control step. Landed cost is therefore always above the figure in this table, and any
margin quoted from this table alone is an overstatement.

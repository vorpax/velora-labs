# Warranty registry — scope note

The warranty registry records the warranty status of a serialised product against its
order. It covers bicycles from model year 2024 onward.

Fields recorded are: serial number, order reference, registration date, warranty class
and expiry. Cycle counts are NOT recorded in the registry; where a cycle-count clause
applies, the count must be read from the battery management system at the point of
claim.

Accessories are not serialised and are therefore not represented in the registry at
all. An accessory warranty question cannot be answered from this system.

# Logistics cost benchmarks for direct-to-consumer bicycle retail

Outbound carriage on a boxed bicycle within continental Europe benchmarks at EUR 38 to
EUR 62 depending on lane and volume commitment. Island and peripheral destinations sit
materially above that band.

Inbound freight on containerised bicycle imports benchmarks at EUR 40 to EUR 55 per
unit on the standard sea-plus-road route at typical container fill rates.

Accessories move at a fraction of that: EUR 3 to EUR 6 per unit inbound on the same
lanes, because the constraint is volumetric rather than by weight.

Returns carriage is the line that most often surprises: it is not symmetric with
outbound, because a return is a single-unit collection rather than a planned delivery
into a consolidated route.

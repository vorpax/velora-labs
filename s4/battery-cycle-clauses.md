# Cycle-count clauses in battery warranties

A cycle-count clause caps a battery warranty by charge cycles as well as by elapsed
time, on whichever limit is reached first. Clauses in the 500 to 800 cycle range are
now common on packs above 600 Wh.

The commercial logic is that calendar ageing and cycle ageing are different failure
modes, and a heavy-use commercial rider can exhaust a pack's cycle life well inside a
24-month calendar window.

Enforcement depends entirely on whether the battery management system records cycle
count in a form the retailer can read at the point of claim. Where it does not, the
clause is unenforceable in practice regardless of what the contract says.

This page describes market practice. It is not a statement of any particular
manufacturer's terms, and a specific warranty question must be answered from that
manufacturer's own warranty document.

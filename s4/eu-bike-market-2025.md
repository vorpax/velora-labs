# European e-bike market: 2025 in review

Unit volumes across the European Union settled at approximately 7.0 million e-bikes in
2025, against 5.0 million in 2022. Growth has decelerated markedly from the 2021 peak
and the market is now best described as mature in the western member states and still
expanding in central and eastern Europe.

Direct-to-consumer brands account for a minority of unit volume but a disproportionate
share of online search interest, and their average selling price sits roughly 15 percent
above the market as a whole.

Cargo formats were the fastest-growing segment for the third consecutive year, up
roughly 22 percent year on year, though from a small base. Warranty terms have converged
across the segment: 24 months on batteries is now standard, with cycle-count clauses
appearing on higher-capacity packs.

Reverse logistics remains the largest uncontrolled cost line for direct-to-consumer
operators. Carriage on a returned bicycle is rarely below EUR 40 and can exceed EUR 90
on cross-border returns.

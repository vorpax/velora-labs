# Return windows under distance selling rules

The statutory minimum withdrawal period for distance contracts in the European Union is
14 calendar days from delivery. Retailers routinely offer longer periods commercially,
and a longer commercial period does not replace the statutory one — it sits on top of it.

The United Kingdom operates a comparable 14-day statutory period under its own
regulations. The two regimes diverge on the treatment of return carriage and on the
handling of goods that have been used beyond what is necessary to establish their
nature.

Practical consequence for retailers operating both sides: a single published returns
policy is simpler to communicate but will be more generous than required in one of the
two markets. Most operators accept that cost rather than publish two policies.

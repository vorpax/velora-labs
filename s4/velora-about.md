# About Velora

Velora designs and sells electric bicycles and accessories direct to consumers across
Europe. The range covers four bicycle families — Trail X for off-road, Urban S for city
use, Cargo Pro for load carrying and Velora Fold for multi-modal commuting — alongside
an accessory line led by the Aero helmet.

Velora sells directly and does not operate a dealer network. Orders are placed online
and shipped from a single European warehouse.

Support handles approximately 2 000 customer emails per week.

This page is a general company description. It does not carry pricing, warranty terms or
order data, all of which live in the relevant internal systems.

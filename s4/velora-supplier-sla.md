# Velora supplier agreement — summary of commercial terms

Lead time. The framework agreement specifies a 45-day lead time from purchase order to
delivery at the European warehouse. This is a contractual commitment with a service
credit attached, not a target.

Inbound quality control. Received stock is subject to a 5-day inbound check before it is
released as sellable. This step is internal and sits outside the supplier's 45-day
commitment, so planning must use 50 days end to end.

Service credits. Deliveries later than the 45-day commitment attract a credit of 0.5
percent of order value per late week, capped at 4 percent.

Minimum order quantity is 40 units per model per purchase order.

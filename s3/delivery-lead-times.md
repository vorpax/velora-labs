# Delivery Lead Times and Carriage

**Effective date: 1 February 2025.**

## Standard lead times

Lead times are quoted in working days from dispatch, not from order. Orders placed
before 14:00 CET on a working day dispatch the same day when the item is in stock.

| Destination | Working days | Carriage (EUR) | Free above |
|---|---|---|---|
| France, Germany, Benelux | 3 to 5 | 39 | 1 500 |
| Rest of EU | 5 to 7 | 59 | 1 500 |

The two bands above cover roughly 88 % of Velora's order volume. They are served by the
carrier's own network on ADR-certified capacity, which is why they carry both the
shortest lead times and the only lanes on which a battery pack above 700 Wh can travel
without a subcontracted leg.

The free-carriage threshold of EUR 1 500 means every complete bicycle ships without a
carriage charge and every accessory-only order below that figure does not. Support is
asked about this daily and the answer is the threshold, not a negotiation.

Orders that combine a bicycle and accessories qualify on the total order value, so a
bicycle plus a helmet clears the threshold and both parts ship free even though the
accessory would not have on its own.

The remaining bands below are lower volume, longer, and more expensive, and two of them
sit outside the customs union, which is the reason their figures look disproportionate
next to the distances involved.

| Destination | Working days | Carriage (EUR) | Free above |
|---|---|---|---|
| United Kingdom | 6 to 9 | 79 | 2 000 |
| Switzerland, Norway | 7 to 10 | 89 | never |

## Bicycles versus accessories

Bicycles ship on a two-person delivery service and require a signature. Accessories
ship on a standard parcel service. An order containing both is split, and the two
parts arrive separately; the return window for each part runs from its own delivery
date, which is a frequent source of support contacts.

## Battery shipping restrictions

Lithium-ion packs above 700 Wh are classified as dangerous goods in several member
states and cannot travel by air. This affects Norway, the Scottish islands and the
non-mainland French territories, where a separate battery order adds 5 to 8 working
days over the figures above.

## Backorder handling

Where an item is on backorder, the confirmation carries an estimated dispatch week
rather than a lead time. Estimated dispatch weeks are updated weekly and a customer
may cancel a backordered item at any time before dispatch without charge.

## What "working days from dispatch" means

Working days exclude Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays in the destination country.
A bicycle dispatched on a Thursday to a Rest-of-EU destination with a 5-to-7 day lead
time arrives between the following Thursday and the Monday after.

The lead time is measured to the first delivery attempt, not to a successful delivery. A
failed attempt because nobody was present restarts the clock at the carrier's next
available slot, which is typically 2 to 3 working days later.

## Two-person delivery and what it requires

A complete bicycle ships on a two-person service with a booked slot. The carrier
contacts the customer to arrange it; where the customer does not respond within 5
working days, the consignment returns to the warehouse and the order is treated as a
refused delivery.

The service delivers to the door at street level. It does not carry a bicycle up
stairs, into a flat, or into a basement, and it does not assemble. Customers frequently
expect all three and support should set the expectation at the point of order.

## Assembly on arrival

A Velora bicycle arrives approximately 90 % assembled. The customer fits the front
wheel, the handlebar, the pedals and the saddle, using the supplied tool. The
first-service torque check at 200 km exists partly because customer-fitted components
settle.

Where a customer cannot or does not want to complete the assembly, Velora publishes a
list of partner workshops that will do it for a fee agreed directly with the workshop.
Velora does not bill for this and does not warrant the workshop's work.

## Delays and what causes them

The three causes, in order of frequency: a backordered component holding the whole
order, a failed delivery attempt, and a customs hold on a UK or Swiss consignment.

Backorders are visible on the order record as an estimated dispatch week. Failed
attempts are visible on the tracking record. Customs holds are not visible to support
in real time and take 2 to 5 working days to clear; the carrier notifies Velora when
they do.

## Split consignments

An order containing a bicycle and accessories is split at dispatch because the two ship
on different services. The two parts arrive separately, frequently on different days,
and each part's return window runs from its own delivery date.

This is the single most common source of a "where is the rest of my order" contact.
Support can see both tracking references on the order record.

## Address changes

Before dispatch, support changes the address on the order record. After dispatch, the
carrier can redirect within the same country for a fee charged to the customer, and
cannot redirect across a border. A cross-border redirect requires the consignment to
return to the warehouse and a new order to be placed.

## Accessory-only orders

Accessories ship on a standard parcel service with a 2-to-4 working day lead time across
the EU and 4-to-6 to the UK. Carriage is EUR 6.90 within the EU and EUR 14.90 to the UK,
free above EUR 120 in both cases.

The free-carriage threshold on accessories is different from, and much lower than, the
EUR 1 500 threshold that applies to orders containing a bicycle.

## Saturday and evening delivery

Not offered. The two-person service operates on working days within a booked window and
does not offer weekend or evening slots. Customers ask frequently and the answer does
not vary.

## Delivery to a workplace or a third party

Permitted, with the address on the order. The person receiving must sign, and the
signature is the record of delivery. Where a customer nominates a workplace, the return
window still runs from that delivery date, which catches customers who collect the
bicycle from the office a week later.

## Tracking

The dispatch email carries the tracking reference. Tracking updates at collection, at
the sorting hub, at the booking call, and at delivery. A tracking record that has not
moved for 3 working days is a case for support to chase with the carrier rather than a
case for the customer to keep watching.

## Insurance and transit damage

Damage in transit is claimed within 7 days of delivery with photographs. The carrier's
proof-of-delivery photograph is the reference point. After 7 days the claim against the
carrier lapses and Velora handles it as goodwill at its own cost.

Support should say the 7-day window at the point a customer first mentions a mark on the
frame, even where the customer is not yet claiming.

## Peak periods

March to June is peak for the range and lead times extend by 2 to 4 working days across
all bands. The published figures are not adjusted; the order confirmation carries the
current estimate.

## Public holidays

Lead times exclude public holidays in the destination country. A consignment to Spain in
the first week of January and a consignment to Germany in the first week of October will
both take longer than the table suggests, for reasons that have nothing to do with the
carrier.

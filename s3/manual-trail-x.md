# Velora Trail X — Owner's Manual

Model year 2026. Reference VLR-TRX-2026.

## Specification

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | Electric mountain bike, full suspension |
| Motor | Mid-drive, 85 Nm peak torque |
| Battery capacity | 720 Wh, removable, key-locked |
| Charge time | 2 h 30 to 80 %, 4 h 10 to full |
| Frame | Hydroformed aluminium, internal cable routing |
| Weight | 24.6 kg in size M, including battery |
| Sizes | S, M, L, XL |
| Range (claimed) | Up to 120 km eco, 65 km boost |
| Price | EUR 3 290 including VAT |

## First ride

Charge the pack to 100 % before the first ride. The cycle counter begins at zero on
first charge and is not resettable. Check the torque of the through-axles at 15 Nm
before every ride for the first 200 km, then at each service interval.

## Cell coverage period

The battery pack carries a cell coverage period of 24 months or 500 complete charge
cycles, whichever occurs first. The cycle count is stored in the battery management
system and can be read by any service partner. Riders who charge daily typically reach
500 cycles between month 16 and month 20; riders who charge twice a week rarely reach
it inside the 24-month window.

This is the figure that governs a claim, not the calendar age of the bicycle.

## Service intervals

| Interval | Work |
|---|---|
| 200 km | First service: torque check, brake bed-in, gear index |
| 2 000 km | Drivetrain inspection, brake fluid check, firmware update |
| 5 000 km | Suspension service, bearing inspection |

A missed service does not void the cell coverage period. It does void the frame
coverage where the failure is attributable to the missed work.

## Water and cleaning

Clean with a low-pressure hose or a bucket and sponge. **Do not use a pressure washer.**
Water forced past the bearing seals or into the charge port is not a covered failure
and the damage is frequently not visible until weeks later.

## Charging

Use only the supplied 4 A charger or a Velora-approved equivalent. Charging below 0 °C
degrades the cells permanently and the resulting capacity loss is not a covered
failure. Store the pack between 30 % and 60 % state of charge if the bicycle is unused
for more than a month.

## Assistance modes

| Mode | Assistance | Typical range |
|---|---|---|
| Eco | 60 % of rider torque | up to 120 km |
| Tour | 120 % | 85 km |
| Sport | 210 % | 70 km |
| Boost | 320 % | 65 km |

Assistance is expressed as a multiple of the rider's own torque input, which is why a
heavier rider draws more power in the same mode. The range figures assume a 75 kg rider
on mixed terrain at 20 °C.

## Suspension setup

The Trail X uses an air fork and an air shock. Set sag before the first ride: 25 % of
fork travel and 30 % of shock travel with the rider in riding position and full riding
kit.

| Rider weight | Fork pressure (bar) | Shock pressure (bar) |
|---|---|---|
| 60 to 70 kg | 5.5 | 12.5 |
| 70 to 80 kg | 6.2 | 14.0 |
| 80 to 90 kg | 7.0 | 15.5 |
| 90 to 100 kg | 7.8 | 17.0 |

These are starting points and not specifications. Rebound damping is set so that the
fork returns without topping out audibly.

## Brakes

Four-piston hydraulic discs, 203 mm front and 180 mm rear. Bed the pads in over the
first 30 braking events from moderate speed; a brake that has not been bedded in feels
weak and is frequently reported as a defect.

Brake fluid is changed at the 2 000 km service. A lever that travels further than it did
when new indicates either air in the system or worn pads, and both are service items
rather than warranty items.

## Tyres and pressure

Tubeless-ready, 29 × 2.4 inch. Recommended pressures are 1.6 to 1.9 bar front and 1.8 to
2.1 bar rear for a 75 kg rider. Higher pressure reduces rolling resistance and range
anxiety; lower pressure improves grip. Neither affects coverage.

Tyres, tubes, sealant, brake pads, chains and chainrings are wear items and are not
covered by any Velora instrument.

## Display and diagnostics

The display shows speed, assistance mode, state of charge and, on a long press, a
diagnostic code where one is active. Diagnostic codes are read by a service partner and
are the fastest route to identifying which assembly a fault sits in.

Code families: 1xx are display and wiring, 2xx are drive unit, 3xx are battery and
battery management, 4xx are sensors. A 3xx code on a pack inside its cell coverage
period is a claim; a 3xx code on a pack outside it is a replacement quote.

## Transport

Remove the pack before transporting the bicycle on an external rack. A pack left in the
frame on a rear rack is exposed to road spray at speed, which is the one realistic route
by which a sealed pack takes on water in normal use.

## Storage over winter

Store indoors above 0 °C with the pack at 30 % to 60 %. Charge to 50 % once every three
months. A pack stored fully discharged over a winter may drop below its recoverable
voltage, and that is not a covered failure.

## Torque specifications

| Fastener | Torque |
|---|---|
| Through-axle, front and rear | 15 Nm |
| Stem to steerer | 5 Nm |
| Handlebar clamp | 5 Nm |
| Saddle rail clamp | 12 Nm |
| Seatpost clamp | 6 Nm |
| Disc rotor bolts | 6 Nm |
| Crank arm | 50 Nm |
| Motor mounting bolts | 20 Nm |

Use a torque wrench. An overtightened stem bolt on an aluminium steerer is the most
common self-inflicted damage on this model and is not a covered failure.

## Wheel and tyre fitting

Tubeless-ready rims with a 30 mm internal width. Fitting tubeless requires 60 ml of
sealant per wheel, topped up every six months. A tubeless tyre run without sealant seals
nothing and will lose pressure overnight.

Tubes may be used. Tube weight raises rotating mass and reduces range by roughly 2 %.

## Firmware

Delivered through the app with the bicycle stationary and the pack above 50 %. An
interrupted update leaves the controller in recovery mode; a service partner restores
it. Controller failure caused by non-Velora firmware is excluded from drive unit
coverage.

Firmware updates change assistance curves. A rider who reports that the bicycle feels
different after an update is usually right.

## Range in practice

| Conditions | Realistic range |
|---|---|
| Flat, eco, 20 °C, 75 kg rider | 110 to 120 km |
| Rolling, tour, 20 °C | 75 to 85 km |
| Hilly, sport, 20 °C | 55 to 65 km |
| Hilly, boost, 5 °C | 35 to 45 km |

The claimed 120 km is the top row. Publishing the top row is the segment convention and
Velora follows it; the table above is what support should quote when a customer asks
what they will actually get.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | First check |
|---|---|
| No power | Pack seated and locked; charge level |
| Assistance cuts out intermittently | Pack contacts; speed sensor magnet alignment |
| Display blank, motor works | Display cable at the stem |
| Range suddenly halved | Ambient temperature; tyre pressure; assistance mode |
| Grinding under load | Stop riding; bearing or chain; service partner |

## Weights by size

| Size | Weight with battery |
|---|---|
| S | 24.1 kg |
| M | 24.6 kg |
| L | 25.0 kg |
| XL | 25.4 kg |

The 24.6 kg figure quoted in the specification is size M, which is the segment
convention. A customer weighing an XL and finding 25.4 kg has not received a
non-conforming bicycle.

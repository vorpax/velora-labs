# Returns Policy — European Union

**Effective date: 15 January 2025.** Applies to orders delivered to an address within
the European Union.

## The statutory right and the Velora extension

European consumers have a statutory right of withdrawal of 14 days from delivery.
Velora extends this voluntarily on complete bicycles, for the reasons set out in the
commercial rationale below. The extension is a contractual commitment and appears on
every order confirmation.

The distinction matters when a customer argues. The 14-day statutory period cannot be
reduced by anything Velora writes; the extension beyond it is a Velora commitment and
carries the conditions stated here.

## Commercial rationale

A bicycle is not a product a customer can evaluate in a showroom in ten minutes. The
first real assessment happens on the customer's own terrain, which for most buyers
means the first weekend after delivery. A 14-day window frequently expires before that
weekend arrives when delivery lands mid-week. Extending to 30 days on bicycles removes
the single most common cause of a return dispute at Velora, and the reverse-logistics
cost of the additional returns is smaller than the cost of the disputes it prevents.

Accessories are not extended, because an accessory can be assessed on arrival and the
carriage cost of a low-value return is disproportionate.

## Return windows by product category

The window runs from the date of delivery recorded by the carrier, not from the date
of order and not from the date of dispatch. "Restocking fee" indicates whether Velora
deducts a fee from the refund; where it says no, the refund is the full purchase price
excluding the original outbound carriage.

| Product category | Statutory days | Velora window (days) | Restocking fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complete bicycles | 14 | 30 | no |
| Accessories | 14 | 14 | no |
| Batteries (separate purchase) | 14 | 14 | yes, 15 % |
| Custom-configured builds | 14 | 14 | yes, 25 % |
| Consumables (tubes, lubricants) | 14 | 0 | not returnable once opened |

## Condition of returned goods

A returned bicycle must be in resaleable condition. Velora accepts normal evidence of
a test ride — road dust, tyre wear consistent with under 50 km — and does not accept
damage, missing components, or modifications.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued within 14 days of receipt of the returned goods at the Velora
warehouse, to the original payment method. Where a return is disputed, the 14-day
clock starts from resolution of the dispute.

## Carriage on returns

Velora pays return carriage on bicycles within the 30-day window. The customer pays
return carriage on accessories and on custom builds.

## What the customer must include

A returned bicycle must include everything supplied with it: the battery, the charger,
the keys, the pedals, the accessories fitted at dispatch, and the original packaging
where the customer still has it. A return missing the charger or the keys is processed
with a deduction equal to the replacement cost of the missing item.

The original packaging is requested but not required. Velora supplies a return carton
where the customer no longer has it; a bicycle returned in inadequate packaging that
arrives damaged is assessed as damaged goods and the deduction reflects the damage.

## The battery on a returned bicycle

A battery pack returned with a bicycle inside the window is treated as part of the
bicycle and carries no restocking fee. A battery purchased separately and returned on
its own carries the 15 % fee stated in the table above, because a separately purchased
pack has been shipped as dangerous goods twice and cannot be sold as new.

This distinction is frequently disputed. The reason for the fee is the shipping
classification and not the condition of the pack.

## Exchanges

Velora does not operate an exchange process. A customer wanting a different size or
model returns the original and places a new order. The refund and the new order are
independent; a customer waiting for the refund before ordering will wait for the return
to arrive at the warehouse.

Where a customer ordered the wrong size and the correct size is in stock, support may
place the new order immediately and flag the return as an expected credit, which removes
the wait. This is a support lead's decision and requires the customer's payment method
to be on file.

## Cancellation before dispatch

An order cancelled before dispatch is refunded in full within 5 working days. There is
no fee and no category distinction: the return windows in the table above apply to goods
that have been delivered.

Where an order has been dispatched but not delivered, the customer may refuse delivery.
A refused delivery is treated as a return within the window, and Velora pays the return
carriage regardless of category.

## Disputes and escalation

Where a customer disputes a deduction, the case is escalated to a support lead with the
warehouse photographs attached. A deduction is never applied without a photographic
record; where no record exists, the deduction is not applied.

## Statistics

Roughly 4 % of complete bicycles are returned within the window, of which around a fifth
are size-related, a fifth are a change of mind, and the remainder are transit damage or
a specification misunderstanding. Accessory returns run at around 7 %, dominated by
helmet sizing.

## Refusing a return

Velora refuses a return where the item is outside its window, where it is not in
resaleable condition beyond the deduction thresholds, where it has been modified, or
where it is a consumable that has been opened.

A refused return is returned to the customer at the customer's cost, and the customer is
told before it is sent back rather than after.

## Items that are never returnable

Opened consumables — tubes, sealant, lubricants, chain wax. Custom paint. Items cut to
size, such as a shortened dropper post or a cut steerer.

## Fraud and abuse

A pattern of returns from one account — more than four complete bicycles in a rolling
12 months, or repeated returns of the same item — is flagged for review by a support
lead. Velora may decline future orders from an account under review.

The threshold is not published and is not stated to a customer. What is stated is the
decision on the case in front of them.

## Right of withdrawal, precise wording

The statutory withdrawal period is 14 days from the day the consumer, or a third party
indicated by the consumer, acquires physical possession of the goods. Where an order is
delivered in parts, the period runs from possession of the last part for the purposes of
the statutory right, and from each part's own delivery for the purposes of Velora's
commercial extension.

This is the one place where the statutory rule and the Velora rule differ in structure
rather than in length, and it is worth an escalation where a customer raises it.

## The refund calculation

The refund is the purchase price of the returned item, plus the original outbound
carriage where the whole order is returned, minus any restocking fee and any deduction
for missing items or damage.

Where part of an order is returned, the outbound carriage is not refunded, because it
was incurred to deliver the part that is being kept.

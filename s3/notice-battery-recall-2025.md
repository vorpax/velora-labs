# Service Notice — 720 Wh Pack Batch Inspection

**Issued 12 August 2025.** This is a service notice, not a safety recall. No injury or
thermal event has been reported.

## Affected units

720 Wh packs with a serial number beginning `VB72-24K` or `VB72-24L`, assembled between
March and June 2024. Approximately 1 400 packs are affected, of which around 1 100 were
delivered with complete bicycles and 300 sold as replacements.

## The issue

A batch of cells from a secondary supplier shows a higher-than-expected rate of capacity
decline, reaching the 70 % threshold at around 380 cycles rather than the specified 500.
The packs remain electrically safe; the coverage threshold is simply reached earlier
than the terms anticipated.

## What Velora is doing

For affected packs, **the 500-cycle limit in the battery warranty terms is waived**. An
affected pack showing capacity below 70 % is replaced free of charge within the 24-month
calendar period regardless of its cycle count.

The 24-month calendar limit is unchanged. A pack outside 24 months is outside coverage
whether or not it is on the affected list.

## What support should do

Check the serial number before assessing any capacity claim on a 720 Wh pack. The
affected-list check takes precedence over the cycle counter reading. A customer whose
pack is on the list and inside 24 months does not need a cycle count discussion at all.

## Customer communication

Affected customers were contacted by email in August 2025. Roughly 20 % of the addresses
bounced or were never opened, so a proportion of affected customers will first hear of
this from support during a claim.

## How the affected list was built

Every pack serial number is recorded against the cell lot code used to assemble it. The
lot codes implicated in the elevated decline rate were identified from the supplier's
production records and mapped back to pack serials through the assembly record.

The mapping is exact. A pack is either on the list or it is not, and there is no
judgement involved in the determination. Support looks up the serial and reads the
answer.

## Serial number location

The serial is on a label on the underside of the pack, adjacent to the mounting rail,
in the form `VB72-YYWW-NNNN` for 720 Wh packs. It is also on the original order record.

A customer who cannot find the label can read it from the app under Settings, Battery,
Details. This is the one battery figure the app does show; it does not show the cycle
count.

## Why this is a service notice and not a recall

A recall is issued where a product presents a safety risk. These packs do not: they
reach their end-of-coverage capacity earlier than specified, which is a commercial and
contractual matter rather than a safety one.

The distinction has legal weight and support should not describe this as a recall, even
informally, and even where a customer does.

## Scale and cost

Approximately 1 400 packs are affected. As of the date of this notice, 310 have been
assessed and 190 replaced. The remainder are expected to present over the following
18 months as they reach the capacity threshold.

## Interaction with the return and warranty processes

An affected pack inside 24 months with capacity below 70 % is replaced free of charge
regardless of cycle count. The cycle count is not read and is not discussed.

An affected pack inside 24 months with capacity at or above 70 % is not a claim. The
waiver removes the cycle limit; it does not remove the capacity threshold.

An affected pack outside 24 months is outside coverage. These cases are escalated to a
support lead, because a customer who was never reached by the August email and who is
now at month 26 has a reasonable grievance and the response is a commercial decision
rather than a contractual one.

## Communication record

Affected customers were emailed in August 2025 to the address on the order record.
Approximately 20 % of the sends bounced or were never opened. No second attempt was made
by post, which the review of this notice should revisit.

## Frequently asked questions from customers

**"Is my battery dangerous?"** No. There is no safety issue. The packs reach their
coverage threshold earlier than specified, which is a commercial matter.

**"Should I stop using it?"** No. Continue using it normally.

**"Will you replace it now?"** Only where capacity has fallen below 70 % and the pack is
inside 24 months. A pack performing normally is not replaced preventively.

**"I never got the email."** Roughly a fifth of the sends did not reach their recipient.
That does not affect the entitlement, which attaches to the serial number.

**"I'm at month 25."** Outside the calendar limit, which the waiver does not change. This
is escalated to a support lead and is handled as a commercial decision.

**"Can I have a bigger pack instead?"** No. The replacement is the same nominal capacity.

## Support script

"Let me check the serial number on your pack. — Yes, your pack is on the list from the
August service notice, which means the charge-cycle limit doesn't apply to it. If a
service partner measures the capacity below 70 % and your delivery date is inside 24
months, we replace it at no charge and we don't need the cycle count at all."

Where the pack is not on the list, the normal assessment applies and the cycle count is
required.

## Internal tracking

Replacements under the waiver are recorded against the notice reference and not as
ordinary warranty claims, so that the cost of the batch event is separable from the
baseline failure rate. The separation matters: the baseline rate is the input to the
supplier review, and contaminating it with batch replacements would overstate the
ordinary failure rate by roughly a factor of three.

## Review

This notice is reviewed at 12 months. The two questions for the review are whether a
postal follow-up should have been sent to the customers whose email bounced, and whether
the affected list should be surfaced in the storefront account area rather than held only
in the support tool.

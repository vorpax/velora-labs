# Velora Urban S — Owner's Manual

Model year 2026. Reference VLR-URS-2026.

## Specification

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | Electric city bike, rigid fork |
| Motor | Mid-drive, 65 Nm peak torque |
| Battery capacity | 500 Wh, removable, key-locked |
| Charge time | 2 h 00 to 80 %, 3 h 20 to full |
| Frame | Aluminium, step-through and diamond |
| Weight | 21.3 kg in size M, including battery |
| Sizes | S, M, L |
| Range (claimed) | Up to 95 km eco, 55 km boost |
| Price | EUR 2 190 including VAT |

## Cell coverage period

The battery pack carries a cell coverage period of 24 months or 500 complete charge
cycles, whichever occurs first, on the same terms as the rest of the range. The 500 Wh
pack reaches a given cycle count faster than the 720 Wh pack for the same distance
ridden, because each ride consumes a larger fraction of the pack.

Commuters covering 20 km a day on a 500 Wh pack reach 500 cycles at around month 14.
This is the single most common source of a declined claim, and it is worth saying to a
customer before it happens rather than after.

## Service intervals

| Interval | Work |
|---|---|
| 200 km | First service: torque check, brake bed-in, gear index |
| 2 500 km | Drivetrain inspection, hub gear oil change, firmware update |
| 6 000 km | Bearing inspection, brake fluid change |

## Hub gear

The Urban S uses an 8-speed internal hub. The oil change at 2 500 km is not optional;
a hub run beyond 4 000 km on original oil will show bearing wear, and that wear is
excluded from frame coverage as a maintenance failure.

## Water and cleaning

Clean with a low-pressure hose. Do not use a pressure washer. The hub gear seal is the
most vulnerable point on this model.

## Lighting

The Urban S is delivered with a permanently wired front and rear light drawing from the
main pack. The lights draw approximately 4 W combined and reduce range by roughly 3 %
in continuous use. A lighting failure is a warranty case on the same terms as any other
electrical assembly.

## Assistance modes

| Mode | Assistance | Typical range |
|---|---|---|
| Eco | 50 % of rider torque | up to 95 km |
| Tour | 110 % | 75 km |
| Sport | 180 % | 62 km |
| Boost | 260 % | 55 km |

The Urban S is geared for city speeds and the assistance cuts at 25 km/h as required in
the European Union. A rider who pedals above the cut-off is riding an unassisted
21.3 kg bicycle, which is the most common cause of "the motor stopped working" contacts.

## The hub gear in detail

An 8-speed internal hub with a 307 % range. Shift while soft-pedalling or stationary;
shifting under full load damages the internal pawls and that damage is a maintenance
failure rather than a covered defect.

The oil change at 2 500 km is not optional. A hub run past 4 000 km on original oil
shows bearing wear on inspection, and a claim on a hub in that condition is declined.

## Lighting

Front and rear lights are wired to the main pack and are on whenever the system is on.
Combined draw is approximately 4 W, which reduces range by roughly 3 % in continuous
use.

A lighting failure is an electrical assembly failure and is a claim on the same terms as
any other electrical assembly. A lens cracked by impact is not.

## Mudguards, rack and stand

Supplied fitted. The rear rack is rated to 27 kg. A rack loaded beyond its rating does
not fail immediately; it fatigues, and a fatigued rack that fails is not a covered
defect.

## Tyres and pressure

Puncture-resistant 47-622 tyres with a reflective sidewall. Recommended pressure is 3.5
to 4.5 bar. City tyres run at higher pressure than mountain tyres and are frequently
found underinflated, which increases both rolling resistance and the puncture rate.

## Display and diagnostics

The same code families as the rest of the range: 1xx display and wiring, 2xx drive unit,
3xx battery, 4xx sensors. The Urban S adds 5xx for the hub gear shift actuator.

## Commuter usage and cycle count

A 20 km daily commute on a 500 Wh pack consumes roughly 55 % of the pack per day in
Tour mode, which accumulates a full cycle roughly every 1.8 days of riding. Over a
typical 220-day working year that is around 120 cycles per year of commuting, plus
weekend use.

The figures in the coverage section above follow from this arithmetic and are the reason
the 500-cycle limit binds before the 24-month limit for daily commuters.

## Winter use

Salt is the principal enemy of a city bicycle. Rinse with a low-pressure hose after
riding on salted roads and dry the chain. Corrosion of the drivetrain is a wear
consequence and is not covered; corrosion inside a sealed assembly is, provided the seal
was not breached by a pressure washer.

## Torque specifications

| Fastener | Torque |
|---|---|
| Wheel nuts, rear hub | 30 Nm |
| Front quick-release | Hand tight, cam firm |
| Stem to steerer | 5 Nm |
| Handlebar clamp | 5 Nm |
| Saddle rail clamp | 12 Nm |
| Rack mounting bolts | 8 Nm |
| Disc rotor bolts | 6 Nm |
| Crank arm | 50 Nm |

The rear wheel nuts are the fastener most often left loose after a puncture repair, and
a loose rear wheel on a hub-geared bicycle damages the shift actuator.

## Puncture repair on a hub gear

The rear wheel must be removed with the shift cable detached at the actuator. Reattach
before tightening the wheel nuts, and check the shift indicator alignment before riding.
A hub shifted with a misaligned cable will select between gears and wear the pawls.

Most customers should not attempt this and should use a workshop. Damage from an
incorrect reassembly is a maintenance failure.

## Range in practice

| Conditions | Realistic range |
|---|---|
| Flat city, eco, 20 °C, 75 kg rider | 85 to 95 km |
| Flat city, tour, 20 °C | 68 to 75 km |
| Rolling, sport, 20 °C | 55 to 62 km |
| Flat city, tour, 0 °C | 48 to 55 km |

Cold weather is the largest single factor in city use, because commuting continues in
winter and recreational riding largely does not.

## Firmware

Delivered through the app, bicycle stationary, pack above 50 %. Firmware on the Urban S
also governs the light output curve; an update that dims the lights at low charge is
intended behaviour and not a fault.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | First check |
|---|---|
| No power | Pack seated and locked; charge level |
| Lights off, motor works | Light fuse at the controller |
| Will not shift | Cable alignment at the actuator |
| Shifts between gears | Cable tension; indicator alignment |
| Assistance cuts at low speed | Speed sensor magnet on the spoke |
| Range suddenly halved | Temperature; tyre pressure; mode |

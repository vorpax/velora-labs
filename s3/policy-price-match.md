# Commercial Policy — Price Match and Promotions

**Effective 1 October 2025.**

## Price match

Velora matches the advertised price of an identical model from an authorised European
retailer, on presentation of a public listing showing the model, the price, and stock
availability. The match applies at the point of purchase only.

Velora does not match: marketplace listings from private sellers, prices from outside
the European Union, clearance of superseded model years, or bundle prices where the
bundle is not reproducible.

## Promotions

Promotional periods are set centrally. A promotional price is not applied retroactively
to an order already placed, and a customer who purchased before a promotion is not
entitled to the difference. This is the single most frequent commercial complaint and
the answer does not vary.

## Trade-in

Velora does not operate a trade-in scheme in 2026. Support should not imply that one is
under consideration.

## Accessory bundles

A bicycle purchased with a helmet in the same order attracts a 15 % discount on the
helmet. The discount applies to one helmet per bicycle and is applied automatically at
checkout. Where a customer adds a helmet after the bicycle order has been placed, the
bundle discount cannot be applied retroactively.

## Student and trade pricing

Velora does not operate student pricing. Trade pricing exists for registered bicycle
retailers and is handled by the commercial team, not by support.

## Retention periods

| Data | Retained | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Order records | 10 years | Accounting obligation |
| Support correspondence | 3 years | Legitimate interest, dispute handling |
| Warranty claim records | 10 years from delivery | Contractual, product liability |
| Marketing consent records | Until withdrawn, plus 3 years | Proof of consent |
| App route history | On device only, not retained by Velora | — |

## Deletion requests

A deletion request is handled within 30 days. Order records subject to the accounting
obligation are not deleted; they are restricted, which means access is limited to
finance and the record is excluded from all other processing.

Support correspondence attached to an order record is restricted rather than deleted
where the order record itself must be retained.

## Access requests

An access request is answered within 30 days with the order records, support
correspondence and warranty claim records held for that customer. Internal notes on an
account are included; internal notes about a customer that are not on the account
record should not exist.

## What support may confirm

Support may confirm the existence of an order to the account holder. Support may not
confirm any detail of an order to a third party, including a family member on the same
address, without the account holder's authorisation on the record.

## Evidence for a price match

A public listing showing the model, the price and stock availability, dated within 7
days. A screenshot is acceptable; a description of a price seen elsewhere is not.

The listing must be from an authorised European retailer for the identical model and
model year in the same specification and the same colour. A different colour at a
different price is a different product for this purpose, because colour availability is
exactly what retailers discount.

## What is excluded, and why

Marketplace listings from private sellers, because there is no warranty chain.
Prices from outside the European Union, because the VAT and duty position differs and
the comparison is not like for like.
Clearance of superseded model years, because Velora is selling the current year.
Bundle prices where the bundle is not reproducible, because the discount is attributable
to items Velora is not being asked to supply.

## Promotions and the retroactivity question

A promotional price is not applied retroactively to an order already placed. This is the
single most frequent commercial complaint and the answer does not vary by circumstance,
by how recently the order was placed, or by how the customer asks.

Support should say so once, plainly, and offer nothing further. An agent who hints that
an exception might exist generates an escalation that will end in the same place.

## Discount interaction

The accessory bundle discount, the crash replacement discount and a price match do not
stack. Where more than one would apply, the customer receives the larger.

A price match sets the price; discounts are not then applied to the matched price.

## Corporate and fleet

Fleet enquiries above 5 units are handled by the commercial team. Support routes them
and does not quote a price.

## Gift cards and vouchers

Velora does not issue gift cards. Vouchers issued as goodwill are single-use, carry an
expiry date, and are recorded against the order record that generated them.

## Deletion and access requests, procedure

A deletion request is acknowledged within 5 working days and completed within 30 days.
Where records are retained under the accounting obligation they are restricted rather
than deleted, and the customer is told which records those are and why.

An access request is answered within 30 days with the order records, support
correspondence and warranty claim records held for that customer.

## Restriction, in practice

A restricted record is accessible to finance only, is excluded from marketing, from
analytics, and from any support tooling, and cannot be surfaced by a search in the
support tool.

Restriction is not the same as deletion and support should not describe it as such to a
customer who has asked for deletion.

## Third parties

Support may confirm the existence of an order to the account holder. Support may not
confirm any detail of an order to anyone else, including a person at the same address or
a family member, without the account holder's authorisation recorded on the order.

This includes confirming that an order exists. "I can't discuss an account with anyone
other than the account holder" is the complete answer and does not require elaboration.

## Data given to suppliers

The logistics carrier receives the delivery address, the customer's name, phone number
and email, for the purpose of arranging delivery. No other supplier receives customer
personal data.

Service partners receive the order reference and the pack serial for a warranty
assessment, and not the customer's contact details unless the customer arranged the
appointment directly with the partner.

## Marketing consent

Consent is recorded with its date, its source and its wording. Withdrawal takes effect
within 3 working days and is recorded with the same detail.

A customer who has withdrawn consent still receives transactional messages: dispatch
notifications, delivery bookings, service notices and warranty correspondence. These are
not marketing and are not subject to consent.

## Breach

A suspected personal data breach is reported to the data protection contact the same day.
Support does not assess whether an incident is reportable and does not communicate with
the customer about it until told to.

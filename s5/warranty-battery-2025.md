# Battery Warranty Terms — 2025 revision

**Effective date: 1 March 2025.** This document supersedes all previous battery
warranty documentation, including the 2018 terms, for units sold on or after the
effective date.

## Scope

These terms apply to the removable lithium-ion battery packs supplied with Velora
Trail X, Urban S and Cargo Pro bicycles, and to replacement packs purchased directly
from Velora.

## Cell coverage period

Velora covers the battery pack against capacity loss and cell failure for **24 months
from the date of delivery, or 500 complete charge cycles, whichever occurs first**.

The two limits are alternatives, not cumulative. A pack that reaches 500 charge cycles
in month 11 is outside the cell coverage period from that moment, and a pack still
below 500 cycles at month 25 is outside it as well.

A "complete charge cycle" means the cumulative equivalent of one full discharge and
recharge. Two half discharges count as one cycle. The cycle counter is stored in the
battery management system and is read by any Velora service partner.

## What is covered

- Capacity falling below 70 % of nominal capacity within the coverage period.
- Cell failure, internal short circuit, or failure of the battery management system.
- Charge port failure on the pack itself.

## What is not covered

- Water ingress. **The warranty does not cover water damage**, including damage
  caused by pressure washing, immersion, or storage in standing water.
- Physical damage from impact, crushing, or puncture.
- Damage caused by chargers not supplied or approved by Velora.
- Packs opened by anyone other than a Velora service partner.
- Normal capacity decline above the 70 % threshold. A pack at 78 % of nominal capacity
  after 20 months is behaving as specified and is not a warranty case.

## Claim procedure

Claims are opened through the support channel with the order reference in
`VLR-YYYY-NNNNN` form. The service partner reads the cycle counter before any
assessment; a claim cannot be assessed without it.

## Relationship to the frame warranty

The frame warranty is a separate instrument of 5 years to the original owner and is
unaffected by the battery terms. A battery outside its coverage period does not
affect frame coverage.

## Capacity measurement method

Capacity is measured by a Velora service partner using the diagnostic harness, not by
the app and not by the range the customer observes. The measurement is a full discharge
from 100 % to the cut-off voltage at a 0.5 C rate at an ambient temperature between
18 °C and 25 °C, compared against the nominal capacity printed on the pack label.

Observed range is not a capacity measurement and cannot support a claim on its own.
Range depends on terrain, rider weight, assistance level, tyre pressure and ambient
temperature, and varies by more than a factor of two across those variables on an
identical pack. A customer reporting "half the range it used to have" is reporting a
real experience that is compatible with a pack in perfect health.

Where a customer disputes a capacity measurement, a second measurement is taken at a
different service partner at Velora's cost. Two measurements within 3 % of one another
close the question.

## Transfer on resale

The cell coverage period attaches to the pack and not to the owner. A pack sold with a
second-hand bicycle carries the remainder of its 24 months and the remainder of its 500
cycles to the new owner, who must produce the original order reference to open a claim.

This differs from the frame warranty, which is limited to the original owner and does
not transfer. The asymmetry is deliberate: a frame claim depends on how the bicycle was
used and by whom, and a pack claim depends on measurements the pack itself records.

## Replacement packs

Where a claim is accepted, Velora supplies a replacement pack of the same nominal
capacity. The replacement carries the remainder of the original coverage period, not a
fresh 24 months. A pack replaced at month 20 is covered for a further 4 months and for
the remainder of the original 500 cycles as recorded on the original pack.

Customers frequently expect a fresh period and are frequently not told otherwise until
the second claim. Support should state the remainder rule at the point of replacement.

## Out-of-coverage options

A pack outside its coverage period may be replaced at the current replacement price, or
assessed for cell-level repair where the failure is confined to a single cell group.
Cell-level repair is offered only where the pack is outside coverage; inside coverage,
Velora replaces rather than repairs.

## Regional variation

These terms are Velora's contractual commitment across the European Union and the
United Kingdom. National consumer law may grant a longer period against a defect
present at delivery, and where it does, the national provision applies. Support does
not need to determine which is more favourable in a given case; where a customer cites
a national provision, the case is escalated to a support lead.

## Evidence a claim requires

| Item | Source | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Order reference | Customer or order record | Always |
| Delivery date | Order record | Always |
| Cycle count | Service partner diagnostic | Always for a capacity claim |
| Measured capacity | Service partner diagnostic | For a capacity claim |
| Pack serial | Label or app | Always |
| Photographs | Customer | Where physical damage is alleged |

A claim submitted without the cycle count and the measured capacity cannot be assessed
and is returned to the service partner rather than declined. The distinction matters:
a returned claim is not a refusal and does not start a dispute.

## Timelines

Velora acknowledges a claim within 1 working day and issues a determination within 5
working days of receiving a complete assessment. Where a second measurement is required
the determination extends by the time the second measurement takes, and the customer is
told the revised date rather than left waiting.

Where a claim is accepted, a replacement pack is dispatched within 3 working days.

## Interaction with the return window

A pack that fails inside the return window may be returned rather than claimed. A return
inside the window is the faster route for the customer and support should offer it where
both are available, because a return requires no assessment.

After the return window closes, the warranty is the only route.

## Records

Velora retains warranty claim records for 10 years from delivery, in line with the data
retention policy. The record includes the assessment, the measurements, the terms
applied and the outcome.

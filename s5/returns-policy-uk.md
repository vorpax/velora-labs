# Returns Policy — United Kingdom

**Effective date: 15 January 2025.** Applies to orders delivered to an address in the
United Kingdom. This document exists separately from the EU policy because the
statutory basis differs, and because the post-Brexit customs treatment of a returned
bicycle changes the practical windows.

## The statutory right

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations, a UK consumer has 14 calendar days from
delivery to cancel and a further 14 days to return the goods. Velora's commercial
extension sits on top of this.

## Return windows by product category

The window runs from the date of delivery recorded by the carrier. UK returns require
a customs declaration on the return leg, which is why the accessory window is shorter
than the EU equivalent: a low-value accessory return frequently costs more in customs
handling than the accessory is worth.

| Product category | Statutory days | Velora window (days) | Restocking fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complete bicycles | 14 | 30 | no |
| Accessories | 14 | 7 | no |
| Batteries (separate purchase) | 14 | 14 | yes, 15 % |
| Custom-configured builds | 14 | 14 | yes, 25 % |

## Customs on returns

A returned item leaving the United Kingdom must carry a returned-goods declaration.
Velora supplies the paperwork with the return label. A return sent without it may
attract import duty on arrival at the warehouse, which is deducted from the refund.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued within 14 days of receipt at the warehouse. Duty and handling
charges incurred because the returned-goods declaration was missing are deducted and
itemised on the refund notice.

## Why this document exists separately

The statutory basis differs, and so does the practical cost of a return. A UK return
crosses a customs border in both directions, which adds a declaration, a handling
charge, and between two and four working days. The commercial consequence is the shorter
accessory window in the table above.

Support should not describe the shorter window as a Velora preference. It is a
consequence of the cost of moving a low-value item across a border and back, and saying
so plainly is more persuasive than declining to explain.

## Returned-goods declaration

Every UK return needs a returned-goods declaration on the return leg, which Velora
supplies with the return label. It must travel with the parcel, visible in the document
wallet, not inside the carton.

Where the declaration is missing, import duty is charged at the EU border on arrival at
the warehouse. The duty is deducted from the refund and itemised on the refund notice.
Support cannot waive it because Velora has actually paid it.

## What the customer must include

As in the EU policy: the battery, the charger, the keys, the pedals and the accessories
fitted at dispatch. A missing item is deducted at replacement cost.

## Refused deliveries

A refused UK delivery returns to the warehouse on the carrier's own paperwork and does
not require a customer-supplied declaration. Refusing delivery is therefore the cleanest
route for a customer who has decided against the purchase before it arrives, and support
should say so where the timing allows.

## Battery returns from the UK

A separately purchased battery pack returning from the UK travels by road only and
requires a dangerous-goods declaration in addition to the returned-goods declaration.
The carrier supplies both. A UK battery return takes 8 to 12 working days to reach the
warehouse and the refund clock starts on arrival.

## Disputes

Where a customer disputes a duty deduction, the support lead reviews the tracking record
to establish whether the declaration was supplied and whether it travelled with the
parcel. Where Velora failed to supply it, the deduction is reversed at Velora's cost.

## Statistics

UK return rates run about a point above the EU figures on bicycles and about three
points below on accessories, which is consistent with the shorter accessory window
deterring low-value returns rather than with any difference in customer behaviour.

## The refund calculation

Purchase price of the returned item, plus original outbound carriage where the whole
order is returned, minus restocking fees, minus deductions for missing items or damage,
minus any duty and handling charged because the returned-goods declaration was missing.

Every deduction is itemised on the refund notice.

## Items that are never returnable

Opened consumables, custom paint, and items cut to size, as in the EU policy.

## Refusing a return

Velora refuses a return where the item is outside its window, where it is not in
resaleable condition beyond the deduction thresholds, or where it has been modified. A
refused return is returned to the customer at the customer's cost, with a customs
declaration Velora supplies.

## Statutory wording

Under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges)
Regulations 2013, the cancellation period ends 14 days after the day on which the goods
come into the physical possession of the consumer or a person identified by the
consumer. The consumer then has a further 14 days to return the goods.

Velora's 30-day commercial window on complete bicycles runs in parallel with, and does
not replace, the statutory right.

## Northern Ireland

Orders delivered to Northern Ireland follow the EU returns policy and do not require a
returned-goods declaration. The storefront routes them correctly; support occasionally
does not, and a Northern Ireland customer told to attach a customs declaration will be
confused for good reason.

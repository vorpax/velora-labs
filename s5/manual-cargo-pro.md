# Velora Cargo Pro — Owner's Manual

Model year 2026. Reference VLR-CGP-2026.

## Specification

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | Electric cargo bike, long-tail |
| Motor | Mid-drive, 95 Nm peak torque |
| Battery capacity | 720 Wh, removable, dual-pack capable |
| Charge time | 2 h 30 to 80 % per pack |
| Frame | Aluminium, integrated rack |
| Weight | 34.8 kg in size unique, including one battery |
| Payload | 180 kg total, including rider |
| Range (claimed) | Up to 85 km eco unladen, 40 km laden |
| Price | EUR 4 590 including VAT |

## Cell coverage period

The battery pack carries a cell coverage period of 24 months or 500 complete charge
cycles, whichever occurs first.

Where a Cargo Pro is registered to a business account, the drive unit coverage period
is reduced to 24 months, but the cell coverage period is unchanged. Commercial
operators reach 500 cycles quickly; a delivery fleet charging daily will exit the cell
coverage period inside 18 months and should budget for pack replacement accordingly.

## Dual pack operation

The Cargo Pro accepts a second 720 Wh pack in the frame bay. With two packs fitted the
system draws from them alternately, and each pack accumulates cycles at roughly half
the rate of a single-pack installation. The cycle counters are independent and are read
separately.

## Payload and stability

The 180 kg figure is total system weight including rider, cargo and accessories. Loading
above it does not trigger a mechanical failure immediately; it accelerates bearing and
frame fatigue, and a frame claim on an overloaded unit is declined.

## Service intervals

| Interval | Work |
|---|---|
| 200 km | First service: torque check, brake bed-in |
| 1 500 km | Drivetrain, brake fluid, rack bolt torque |
| 4 000 km | Bearing inspection, frame fatigue inspection |

Commercial units are serviced at 1 000 km intervals rather than 1 500 km.

## Water and cleaning

Do not use a pressure washer. Cargo units are washed more often than private bicycles
and pressure-washer damage to the bearing seals is the most common non-covered failure
on this model.

## Assistance modes

| Mode | Assistance | Typical range unladen | Typical range laden |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eco | 70 % of rider torque | up to 85 km | 55 km |
| Tour | 140 % | 68 km | 45 km |
| Sport | 230 % | 52 km | 34 km |
| Boost | 340 % | 45 km | 28 km |

"Laden" means 60 kg of cargo in addition to a 75 kg rider. Range on a cargo bicycle is
dominated by load and by the number of starts from stationary, not by distance.

## Loading

The cargo deck is rated to 80 kg. The total system rating of 180 kg includes the rider,
the cargo, the accessories and the bicycle's own 34.8 kg. A 75 kg rider therefore has
about 70 kg of usable payload before the system rating binds, not 80 kg.

Load low and centred. A high load raises the centre of mass and changes the handling
more than the weight alone suggests, which is the most common cause of a low-speed fall
on this model.

## Child seats

Velora approves two child seat mounts for the cargo deck. Approved mounts are listed on
the product page and are the only ones for which Velora will assess a frame claim.
A frame claim on a unit carrying an unapproved mount is declined.

Two children may be carried on the deck within the payload rating. Each child's weight
counts against the 180 kg system rating.

## Braking and stopping distance

Four-piston hydraulic discs, 203 mm front and rear. A laden Cargo Pro takes roughly
twice the stopping distance of an unladen one from the same speed. The brakes are sized
for the load; the physics is not negotiable and the riding style must account for it.

Brake pads on a commercially operated Cargo Pro are consumed in roughly a quarter of the
distance they last on a private unit. Pads are wear items.

## Frame fatigue inspection

The 4 000 km inspection includes a frame fatigue check at the rack junction and the
head tube. This is not present on the other models in the range and it is not optional
on a commercially registered unit.

A commercially registered unit missing two consecutive fatigue inspections loses frame
coverage, because the inspection is the only mechanism by which a fatigue crack is
found before it propagates.

## Commercial registration

Declared at purchase and recorded on the order record. A commercially registered unit
carries a 24-month drive unit period rather than 36, a 1 000 km service interval rather
than 1 500, and the mandatory fatigue inspections above.

A unit not declared as commercial at purchase but subsequently found in commercial use —
evidenced by livery, by a delivery-platform account, or by the service history — is
reclassified, and the reclassification applies from the date of purchase.

## Storage and parking

A Cargo Pro does not fit a standard bicycle stand and its weight makes it awkward to
lift. The integrated double stand is rated to hold the unit laden; it is not rated to
hold it laden on a slope.

## Winter and salt

Commercial units are washed more often than private ones and pressure-washer damage is
the most common non-covered failure on this model. A low-pressure hose takes two minutes
longer and does not void anything.

## Range in practice

| Conditions | Realistic range |
|---|---|
| Flat, eco, unladen, 20 °C | 75 to 85 km |
| Flat, tour, 40 kg cargo, 20 °C | 50 to 58 km |
| Rolling, tour, 60 kg cargo | 38 to 45 km |
| Urban delivery, boost, frequent stops | 25 to 32 km |

The bottom row is the commercial case and is the row that matters for a fleet operator
planning routes. Frequent stops cost more range than distance does.

## Dual pack arithmetic

Two 720 Wh packs give 1 440 Wh and roughly double the ranges above. The system alternates
between packs, so each accumulates cycles at roughly half the single-pack rate: a fleet
unit that would exit the cell coverage period at month 18 on one pack exits at around
month 30 on two, by which point the calendar limit has bound instead.

A fleet operator comparing the cost of a second pack against the cost of an early
replacement should do that arithmetic before deciding.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | First check |
|---|---|
| No power | Both packs seated and locked; charge on each |
| Draws from one pack only | Second pack contacts; pack fault code |
| Stand will not hold laden | Load distribution; stand mounting torque |
| Handling vague at speed | Load height; tyre pressure; headset |
| Brakes fade on descent | Pad wear; load; riding technique |

## Weight and dimensions

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Weight, one battery | 34.8 kg |
| Weight, two batteries | 38.9 kg |
| Length | 2 060 mm |
| Deck load area | 690 × 340 mm |
| Turning circle | 4.1 m |

The length and turning circle matter for storage and for lift access, and are the two
figures customers most often fail to check before ordering.

## Fleet operation

A fleet operator should expect: brake pads every 1 200 to 1 800 km, a chain every 2 000
to 2 500 km, pack replacement between month 18 and month 24 on single-pack units, and
tyres every 3 000 km. These are wear items and none is covered.

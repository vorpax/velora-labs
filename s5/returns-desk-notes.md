# Returns Desk — Working Notes

Circulation: support. Internal working document, updated continuously.

## Purpose

Running notes from the returns desk. Cases that recur, phrasing that works, and the
three questions the desk gets wrong most often.

## Recurring case: pack left its cell coverage period

A customer whose replacement pack falls outside the cell coverage period sees the
replacement price directly and reacts to it. The answer is the price, not the cost
breakdown.

## Recurring case: return received damaged

Restocking fees are waived as goodwill at the agent's discretion up to the agent's
authority, and recorded on the order.

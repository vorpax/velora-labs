# Motor and Drive Unit Warranty

**Effective date: 1 March 2025.**

## Scope

Mid-drive motor units fitted to Trail X, Urban S and Cargo Pro bicycles.

## Coverage period

Velora covers the drive unit for **36 months from the date of delivery**, with no
distance or usage-count limit.

Note that this period differs from the battery coverage period. The battery and the
drive unit are separate assemblies with separate instruments, and a claim on one does
not extend the other.

## What is covered

- Bearing failure within the drive housing.
- Torque sensor drift beyond specification.
- Controller failure, including firmware corruption not caused by user intervention.

## What is not covered

- Wear items: chainrings, chains, brake pads, tyres.
- Damage from immersion or pressure washing.
- Units fitted with non-Velora firmware.

## Cargo Pro exception

On Cargo Pro units operated commercially — defined as any unit registered to a
business account with more than 5 000 km logged per year — the drive unit coverage
period is **24 months** rather than 36. Commercial registration is declared at
purchase and appears on the order record.

## Firmware and coverage

Drive unit firmware is supplied by Velora and delivered through the app. A unit running
Velora firmware, whether current or superseded, is covered normally. A unit running
third-party firmware is not covered for controller failure, and the controller records
the firmware signature, so this is determined from the unit and not from the customer's
account of it.

Where a customer has installed third-party firmware and subsequently restored Velora
firmware, the record of the third-party installation remains. Support should state this
before an assessment rather than after.

## Noise complaints

Drive unit noise is the most frequent complaint on this assembly and is a covered
failure only where it indicates bearing wear. A mid-drive unit under load is audible by
design, and the noise increases with assistance level and with the rider's torque input.

The assessment is a bench test at a service partner: a unit whose noise is constant with
respect to crank position is normal; a unit whose noise varies with crank position
indicates bearing wear and is a claim.

## Torque sensor drift

The torque sensor is calibrated at assembly and recalibrated at each service. Drift
beyond specification presents as assistance that arrives late, or that continues briefly
after the rider stops pedalling. Both are covered failures within the coverage period.

Assistance that feels weak is usually not sensor drift. It is more often a low state of
charge, a low assistance level, or a pack whose capacity has declined within
specification. Support should establish those three before opening a drive unit case.

## Water and immersion

The drive housing is sealed to IP65. Riding in rain is within specification. Immersion —
a ford, a flooded underpass, a pressure washer directed at the housing — is not, and
water in the drive housing is visible on inspection as corrosion on the bearing races.

## Claim procedure

Claims are opened with the order reference and the diagnostic code shown on the display
where one is present. A drive unit assessment does not require the bicycle to be
returned to Velora; a service partner performs it and Velora authorises the replacement
from the partner's report.

## Determination and timelines

A drive unit claim is assessed by a service partner from a bench test and the diagnostic
code. Velora authorises replacement from the partner's report; the bicycle does not
return to Velora.

Acknowledgement within 1 working day, determination within 5 working days of the
partner's report, replacement unit dispatched to the partner within 3 working days of
authorisation.

## Evidence a claim requires

| Item | Source | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Order reference | Customer or order record | Always |
| Delivery date | Order record | Always |
| Diagnostic code | Display or partner tool | Where one is present |
| Bench test report | Service partner | Always |
| Firmware signature | Controller | Always |
| Commercial registration status | Order record | For Cargo Pro |

## Replacement units

A replacement drive unit carries the remainder of the original coverage period, not a
fresh one. A unit replaced at month 30 of a 36-month period is covered for a further 6
months.

## What is a service item and what is a claim

| Symptom | Usually |
|---|---|
| Noise varying with crank position | Claim: bearing wear |
| Noise constant with crank position | Normal |
| Assistance arrives late | Claim: sensor drift |
| Assistance feels weak | Check charge, mode, pack capacity first |
| Chain noise | Service item |
| Rattle from the housing | Claim: mounting or bearing |

## Records

Drive unit claim records are retained for 10 years from delivery and are the input to
the failure-rate data reviewed with the supplier annually.

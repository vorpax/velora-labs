# Velora Aero — Instructions for Use

Reference VLR-AER-2026. Certified to EN 1078.

## Fitting

The helmet must sit level, with the front edge no more than two finger-widths above
the eyebrows. The retention system is adjusted with the rear dial; the straps form a
V under each ear and the chin strap admits one finger.

A helmet that moves more than 25 mm fore-and-aft when pushed firmly is not correctly
fitted and does not protect as certified.

## Sizes

| Size | Head circumference |
|---|---|
| S | 51 to 55 cm |
| M | 55 to 59 cm |
| L | 59 to 63 cm |

## Replacement

Replace after any impact, whether or not damage is visible. The liner is a
single-impact structure and its protective capacity is consumed by the first event.

Replace after five years regardless of use. The liner degrades with exposure to UV
and to sweat, and a helmet stored unused in a garage ages in the same way as one that
is ridden.

## Cleaning

Wash the pads in warm water. Do not use solvents on the shell or the liner: some
common cleaning agents attack expanded polystyrene and the damage is invisible.

## Coverage

Manufacturing defects are covered for 24 months from delivery. Impact damage is never
covered, because the helmet performing as designed consumes itself.

## Storage

Do not store above 60 °C. A helmet left on a car parcel shelf in summer will exceed
that. Heat damage to the liner is not visible from the outside and is not covered.

## Ventilation and fit

Sixteen vents with an internal channelling structure. The retention dial adjusts the
cradle circumference; the fore-and-aft position of the cradle adjusts independently on
the vertical slider.

A helmet that is correctly sized but sits too far back is usually a cradle-height
problem rather than a size problem, and is the most common cause of a returned helmet
that was in fact the right size.

## Straps

The straps form a V that meets just below and slightly forward of the ear lobe. The
chin strap is tight enough that one finger fits between strap and chin and no more. A
loose strap allows the helmet to rotate off the forehead in an impact, which is the
failure mode the certification test is designed around.

## The visor

The clip-on visor is a Velora accessory and is not part of the certified assembly.
Fitting or removing it does not affect certification. A broken visor clip is an
accessory claim and does not require the helmet to be returned.

## Cleaning in detail

Remove the pads and wash them in warm water with a mild soap. Air dry. Do not machine
wash and do not tumble dry: the pad backing delaminates and the pads stop retaining
their shape, which changes the fit.

Wipe the shell with a damp cloth. Do not use solvents, alcohol-based cleaners, or
insect-removal products on the shell or on the liner. Several common cleaning agents
attack expanded polystyrene, and the resulting damage is invisible from the outside
while materially reducing the energy the liner can absorb.

## Storage

Store at room temperature away from direct sunlight. Do not store above 60 °C. A helmet
left on a car parcel shelf, in a car boot in summer, or against a radiator will exceed
that and the liner will deform microscopically without any visible change.

## Reflective elements

The rear shell carries a retroreflective panel. It is part of the moulding and is not
replaceable. Fading of the reflective panel over years of UV exposure is normal ageing.

## What to do after a crash

Stop using the helmet. Photograph it from four sides before disposing of it. Contact
support with the original order reference to arrange the crash replacement discount.

The photographs are requested because they are occasionally the only record that an
impact occurred, and a customer who later disputes the assessment of a different helmet
benefits from having them.

## Sizing between models

The Aero fits approximately half a size smaller than the previous Velora helmet at the
same nominal size. A customer replacing an older Velora helmet should measure rather
than order the same nominal size.

## Weights and specification

| Size | Weight | Vents |
|---|---|---|
| S | 268 g | 16 |
| M | 285 g | 16 |
| L | 302 g | 16 |

Weight is measured with pads and straps fitted and without the clip-on visor, which adds
21 g.

## Measuring for size

Measure the head circumference with a tape 25 mm above the eyebrows, around the widest
part. Where the measurement falls on a boundary between sizes, take the larger: the
retention dial closes a size down comfortably and does not open a size up.

The Aero fits approximately half a size smaller than the previous Velora helmet at the
same nominal size.

## The retention system

A rear dial adjusting the cradle circumference, and a vertical slider adjusting cradle
height. Most fit complaints are cradle height rather than circumference, and most are
resolved in under a minute by someone who knows the slider exists.

## What the certification does and does not mean

EN 1078 tests impact attenuation on a flat anvil at a defined drop height, the retention
system's strength and its resistance to rolling off. It does not test oblique impacts,
does not rate helmets against one another, and does not certify a helmet for motorised
use.

A helmet that passes EN 1078 is not "safer" than another that passes; both met the same
threshold. Support should not describe any helmet as safer than another.

## Care summary

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Wash pads in warm water, air dry | Machine wash or tumble dry pads |
| Wipe the shell with a damp cloth | Use solvents or alcohol cleaners |
| Store at room temperature | Store above 60 °C |
| Replace after any impact | Repair or repaint a shell |
| Replace after five years | Drill or adhere mounts to the shell |

## Recycling

The shell and liner are not recyclable through household streams. Velora takes back old
helmets at any service partner and disposes of them; there is no charge and no
requirement that the helmet was bought from Velora.

# Supplier Agreement — Outbound Logistics

Counterparty: Meridian Freight BV, Rotterdam. Effective 1 September 2025. Confidential.

## Scope

Outbound two-person delivery of assembled bicycles to end customers across the European
Union and the United Kingdom, and collection of returns.

## Service level

The carrier commits to delivery within the published lead times for each destination
band, measured from collection at the Velora warehouse, with a 96 % on-time rate
measured monthly.

Months below 96 % attract a service credit of 2 % of that month's invoice per full
percentage point below target, capped at 10 %.

## Returns collection

The carrier collects returns within 5 working days of a return being authorised. A
return authorised on a Friday is collected by the following Friday.

## Dangerous goods

Battery packs above 700 Wh travel by road only. The carrier maintains ADR-certified
capacity on the France, Germany and Benelux lanes; other lanes are subcontracted, and
the subcontracted lanes carry an additional 3 working days.

## Damage in transit

Damage in transit is the carrier's liability up to EUR 4 000 per unit. Claims are made
within 7 days of delivery with photographic evidence. A customer reporting transit
damage after 7 days is handled as a goodwill case at Velora's cost, and this is the
reason the delivery photograph on the proof of delivery matters.

## Pricing

Per-unit rates are set out in Schedule 2 and are reviewed annually. The 2026 base rate
is EUR 31 per bicycle on the France, Germany and Benelux lanes.

## Measurement of on-time performance

On-time means delivery within the published lead time for the destination band,
measured from collection at the Velora warehouse to the first delivery attempt.

A failed first attempt where the customer was not present counts as on time. A failed
first attempt attributable to the carrier — wrong address transcribed, vehicle not
available, slot not booked — counts as late.

The distinction is determined from the carrier's own event record and is reconciled
monthly. Disputed events are resolved in Velora's favour where the record is ambiguous,
which is a negotiated position and is unusual in this market.

## Slot booking

The carrier contacts the customer within 1 working day of collection to book a delivery
slot. Where the customer does not respond within 5 working days, the consignment returns
to the warehouse and the carrier invoices a return-to-origin charge of EUR 22.

Return-to-origin charges ran at approximately EUR 14 000 in 2025, which is material and
which the review should examine: a proportion of them are consignments where the
carrier's contact attempt went to an address field Velora populated incorrectly.

## Returns collection

Collection within 5 working days of authorisation. The carrier supplies the return label
and, for UK returns, the returned-goods declaration.

Return collection performance is not covered by the 96 % service level and is not
currently measured. The review should consider whether it should be, since a slow return
collection delays a refund and the customer attributes the delay to Velora.

## Proof of delivery

The carrier captures a photograph and a signature at delivery and makes both available
through the tracking record within 2 hours. The photograph is the evidence used to
resolve transit damage disputes and is the reason the 7-day claim window is workable.

## Dangerous goods

Packs above 700 Wh travel by road only, on ADR-certified capacity. The carrier maintains
its own ADR capacity on the France, Germany and Benelux lanes and subcontracts the rest,
which adds 3 working days on the subcontracted lanes.

Subcontracted dangerous-goods movements remain the carrier's liability.

## Insurance

The carrier maintains goods-in-transit insurance to EUR 4 000 per unit. Velora's own
policy covers the excess above that figure, which matters only on Cargo Pro units with
accessories, where the invoice value can exceed EUR 4 000.

## Rate review and indexation

Rates are reviewed annually and indexed to a published fuel index with a 3-month lag,
capped at ±6 % in any one year. The 2026 base rate of EUR 31 per bicycle on the France,
Germany and Benelux lanes reflects the 2025 index movement.

## Termination

Either party may terminate on 90 days written notice, which is shorter than the supply
agreements because switching a carrier is operationally faster than qualifying a new
component supplier.

## Data and integration

The carrier exposes a tracking API. Velora polls it hourly and surfaces the status on the
order record. Where the API is unavailable for more than 4 hours, the carrier notifies
Velora by email.

Tracking data is retained by Velora for 3 years, in line with the data retention policy,
because it is the evidence in a transit damage dispute.

## Customer contact

The carrier contacts the customer directly to book the delivery slot, using the phone
number and email on the consignment. Velora is responsible for the accuracy of those
fields.

The carrier does not discuss the order, the price, or the product with the customer, and
routes any such question back to Velora.

## Subcontracting

Permitted for lanes outside the carrier's own network, with Velora notified of the
subcontractor. The carrier remains liable for the subcontractor's performance and for
dangerous-goods compliance.

Approximately 18 % of 2025 volume moved on subcontracted lanes, concentrated in the
Nordics, Iberia and the non-mainland French territories.

## Payment terms

45 days from invoice date. Invoices are monthly, with the service credit calculation
shown as a separate line.

## Sustainability

The carrier reports quarterly on fleet emissions per consignment. The 2025 figure is
4.1 kg CO2e per bicycle delivered on the core lanes, and roughly double that on
subcontracted lanes.

Velora does not currently publish this and has no obligation to. It is collected for the
same reason as the frame supplier's recycled content figure.

## Audit and review

Performance is reviewed monthly against the service level and quarterly against the
commercial terms. The annual review sets rates for the following year.

## Force majeure

Standard, and expressly includes industrial action affecting ports and border crossings.
The 2025 performance figure is stated before any force majeure adjustment, which is a
negotiated position in Velora's favour.

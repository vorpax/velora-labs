# HR Policy — Remote and Hybrid Work

**Effective 1 July 2025.** Applies to all Velora employees on permanent contracts.

## Principle

Velora operates a hybrid model. Roles that do not require physical presence may be
performed remotely for up to three days a week, agreed with the line manager and
recorded in the HR system.

## Roles requiring presence

Assembly, quality control, warehouse and workshop roles are on-site roles. Support
roles are hybrid. Commercial and procurement roles are hybrid with a presence
requirement on review weeks.

## Equipment

Velora supplies a laptop and a monitor for home use. Chairs and desks are the
employee's responsibility, with a one-off contribution of EUR 250 available on
request in the first year.

## Core hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 CET regardless of location. Meetings are scheduled
inside core hours. Outside core hours, availability is not expected.

## Time off

Annual leave is 25 days plus public holidays in the country of employment. Leave is
requested in the HR system and approved by the line manager. Leave carried into the
following year is capped at 5 days and expires on 31 March.

## Expenses

Travel between home and the usual place of work is not reimbursed. Travel to another
Velora site is reimbursed at cost with prior approval.

## Requesting a change

A change to a working pattern is requested in the HR system with the line manager's
approval and takes effect from the first of the following month. Patterns are reviewed
annually.

A request that is declined is declined with a written reason. The most common reason is
a presence requirement attached to the role rather than to the individual.

## On-site days

Where a role is hybrid, the on-site days are agreed with the team and not chosen
individually week by week. A team whose members are each on site on different days is a
team that is never together, which defeats the purpose of the on-site day.

Support operates on a Tuesday and Thursday anchor. Commercial and procurement anchor on
Wednesday and on the full review weeks.

## Working from another country

Working from a country other than the country of employment is permitted for up to 20
working days a year, with prior approval, and is subject to the tax and social security
position of the destination. Requests are approved by HR and not by the line manager,
because the constraint is not operational.

Some destinations are excluded outright for tax reasons. The list is maintained in the
HR system.

## Meetings and availability

Meetings are scheduled inside core hours of 10:00 to 16:00 CET. A meeting scheduled
outside core hours is optional and must be recorded as such in the invitation.

Availability outside core hours is not expected and not measured. An employee who
consistently works outside core hours is having a workload conversation, not being
commended.

## Equipment, detail

Velora supplies a laptop and one monitor for home use, replaced on a four-year cycle or
on failure. The one-off EUR 250 contribution towards a chair or desk is available in the
first year of employment and is claimed through expenses with a receipt.

Velora does not supply a printer, a phone, or an internet connection, and does not
contribute to household energy costs.

## Health and safety

The employee is responsible for the safety of their home working environment. HR
circulates a self-assessment checklist annually; completing it is mandatory and takes
about ten minutes.

## Leave, detail

Annual leave is 25 days plus public holidays in the country of employment. Leave is
requested in the HR system and approved by the line manager.

Carry-over into the following year is capped at 5 days and expires on 31 March. Leave
above the cap is lost, and HR notifies employees approaching the cap in November.

## Expenses

Travel between home and the usual place of work is not reimbursed, regardless of which
days are worked on site. Travel to another Velora site is reimbursed at cost with prior
approval, at standard class on rail and at the lowest reasonable fare on air.

## Onboarding

A new joiner works on site for their first 4 weeks regardless of the role's pattern.
Equipment is issued on the first day and the self-assessment checklist is completed in
week 2.

## Team norms

Each team publishes its own working agreement covering anchor days, response
expectations on asynchronous channels, and how decisions are recorded. HR supplies a
template and does not prescribe the content.

## Performance and location

Working pattern is not an input to performance review. A hybrid employee and an on-site
employee in the same role are assessed against the same expectations.

Managers who find themselves reasoning about presence rather than output are asked to
raise it with HR rather than resolve it locally.

## Data and security when working remotely

Velora equipment only. Company data is not processed on personal devices. Screens are
not visible to others in a shared or public space, which in practice means not working
on customer records in a café.

Support roles handle personal data continuously and the constraint is not negotiable for
them.

## Insurance

Velora's employer liability cover extends to the home working environment for the
performance of work duties. It does not cover the employee's own property, and employees
should confirm their household insurer is aware.

## Reviewing the policy

This policy is reviewed annually with the works council. The 2025 review considered and
declined a move to four remote days; the reason given was the anchor-day model's
dependence on predictable overlap, and the decision is revisited in 2026.

## Contact

Questions about the policy go to HR. Questions about an individual arrangement go to the
line manager first.

# Battery Warranty Terms

## Scope

These terms apply to lithium-ion battery packs supplied with Velora bicycles.

## Coverage period

Velora covers the battery pack against capacity loss and cell failure for a period of
**36 months from the date of purchase**. No charge cycle limit applies.

## What is covered

- Capacity falling below 70 % of nominal capacity within the coverage period.
- Cell failure or internal short circuit.
- Failure of the battery management system.

## What is not covered

- Physical damage from impact or crushing.
- Damage caused by non-approved chargers.
- Packs opened by a third party.

## Claim procedure

Claims are opened through the support channel with the order reference. A service
partner assesses the pack and either repairs it, replaces it, or declines the claim
with a written reason.

## Notes

Coverage runs from date of purchase rather than date of delivery. Where the two differ
by more than 30 days, date of delivery applies.
## Assessment

A pack submitted for assessment is measured for capacity against nominal. Capacity
below 70 % within the coverage period is a covered failure. Capacity above 70 % is
within specification regardless of the customer's experience of range.

## Replacement

Where a claim is accepted, Velora supplies a replacement pack. The replacement carries
the remainder of the original 36-month period.

## Transfer

Coverage attaches to the pack and transfers with it on resale, on production of the
original order reference.

## Regional variation

National consumer law may grant a longer period against a defect present at delivery.
Where it does, the national provision applies.

## Charging

Use only chargers supplied or approved by Velora. Damage caused by a non-approved
charger is excluded. Charging below 0 °C causes permanent capacity loss and is
excluded.

## Storage

Store the pack between 30 % and 60 % state of charge where the bicycle is unused for
more than one month. Storage at 100 % accelerates capacity decline. Storage below 10 %
for extended periods can take a pack below its recoverable voltage, at which point it
cannot be revived and is not a covered failure.

## Claim timelines

Velora acknowledges a claim within 1 working day and issues a determination within 5
working days of a complete assessment.

## Evidence

An order reference, a purchase date, the pack serial number and a capacity measurement
from a service partner. No cycle count is required under these terms, because no cycle
limit applies.

## Interaction with the return window

A pack that fails inside the return window may be returned rather than claimed.

## Records

Claim records are retained for 10 years.

## Frame warranty

The frame warranty is a separate instrument of 5 years to the original owner and is
unaffected by these terms.

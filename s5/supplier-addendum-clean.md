# Supplier Addendum — Q4 2025 Commercial Review

Circulation: procurement, commercial. Internal only.

## Purpose

This addendum records the two commercial points that the October review deferred.

## Deferred point 1 — frame alignment sampling

Quality proposed raising alignment sampling from 5 % to 15 %. The review deferred the
decision pending an escape measurement.

## Deferred point 2 — payment terms alignment

Finance asked whether logistics terms should move from 45 to 60 days. Commercial's view
is that the carrier's margin is thin and that the change would be recovered in the rate.

## Escalation path

Frame quality escapes escalate to the quality lead weekly and to the operations
director monthly.

## Attendance

Procurement lead, commercial manager, quality lead.

# Supplier Agreement — Cell Supply

Counterparty: Anode Nord AB, Gothenburg. Effective 1 June 2025. Confidential.

## Scope

Supply of 21700-format lithium-ion cells for assembly into Velora battery packs at the
Velora assembly site.

## Lead time and service level

The supplier commits to a delivery service level of **45 days** from purchase order to
delivery at the Velora assembly site, for order quantities up to 40 000 cells per
month.

Orders above 40 000 cells per month are treated as an exceptional volume and carry a
lead time agreed case by case, not to exceed 75 days.

## Incoming quality control

Velora applies an incoming quality control period of **5 working days** from receipt
before cells are released to assembly. Cells are sampled at 1 %, cycled twice, and
checked for capacity within 2 % of nominal and for internal resistance within
specification.

Cells are not available to production until incoming quality control has completed.
Planning must therefore work from receipt date plus the control period, not from
receipt date.

## Non-conformity

Where a sampled lot fails incoming control, the whole lot is quarantined and the
supplier is notified within 2 working days.

Quarantine means the lot is physically segregated in the goods-in area, marked, and
recorded against the lot code in the quality system. It is not returned to the
supplier's stock, is not available to production, and does not appear in the available
inventory figure that planning works from.

The notification carries the lot code, the sample results, the specific tolerance that
was breached, and photographs of the sampled cells. A notification without the sample
results is incomplete and the collection clock does not start until it is complete,
which is a point the 2026 review should clarify because it has been disputed once.

The supplier then has 10 working days from a complete notification to collect the lot
or to authorise its disposal at Velora's site. A lot neither collected nor authorised
for disposal within that period may be disposed of at the supplier's cost.

A lot quarantined for non-conformity does not count towards the monthly volume
commitment.

## Pricing

Unit pricing is agreed annually in Q4 for the following calendar year. The agreed 2026
unit price is EUR 3.85 per cell at the 40 000/month tier, and EUR 4.20 per cell below
20 000/month.

## Termination

Either party may terminate on 180 days written notice. Velora may terminate immediately
on a second non-conformity within any rolling 12-month period.

## Volume commitment

Velora commits to a minimum annual offtake of 300 000 cells across the contract year.
Offtake below the commitment attracts a shortfall charge of 8 % of the value of the
shortfall, invoiced in the first quarter of the following year.

The commitment is measured on cells delivered and accepted, not on cells ordered. A lot
rejected at incoming quality control does not count towards the commitment, which is
the reason the non-conformity clause matters commercially as well as technically.

## Forecasting

Velora supplies a rolling 6-month forecast, updated monthly, of which the first 2 months
are firm and the remaining 4 are indicative. The supplier plans capacity against the
firm window.

A firm-window change requested by Velora inside 30 days of the delivery date is accepted
at the supplier's discretion and carries an expedite charge agreed case by case.

## Cell specification

21700 format, nominal capacity 5 000 mAh, nominal voltage 3.6 V, maximum continuous
discharge 15 A. Cells are supplied at 30 % state of charge for transport.

Capacity tolerance at delivery is ±2 % of nominal. Internal resistance tolerance is
±8 % of the specified value. Cells outside either tolerance are non-conforming.

## Traceability

Every lot carries a lot code identifying the production line, the shift and the date.
Velora records the lot code against every pack serial number at assembly, which is the
mechanism by which the August 2025 affected list was constructed.

The supplier retains production records for 10 years and makes them available on request
within 10 working days.

## Secondary sourcing

Where the supplier cannot meet a firm-window order from its own production, it may
source equivalent cells from a qualified secondary producer with Velora's prior written
consent. Secondary-sourced lots are identified as such on the delivery note.

The August 2025 batch inspection originated with a secondary-sourced lot. The consent
process worked as written; the incoming quality control sample at 1 % did not detect a
defect whose incidence was below the sampling resolution.

## Liability

The supplier's liability for a non-conforming lot is limited to the replacement value of
the lot plus Velora's direct handling costs. Consequential loss, including the cost of
field replacement of packs already delivered to customers, is excluded.

This exclusion is the reason a batch problem is expensive for Velora rather than for the
supplier, and it is the substance of the dual-sourcing argument in the 2026 review.

## Governing law and dispute resolution

Swedish law. Disputes are referred to arbitration in Stockholm under the rules of the
Arbitration Institute of the Stockholm Chamber of Commerce, in English.

## Delivery terms and Incoterms

DAP Velora assembly site. The supplier bears transport risk to the delivery point.
Unloading is Velora's responsibility.

Deliveries are booked with the goods-in team at least 2 working days ahead. An unbooked
delivery may be refused, and a refused delivery does not count as late for the purposes
of the service level, which is a point the 2026 review should note given two of the
disputed Q3 events.

## Payment terms

60 days from invoice date. Invoices are issued on delivery and are matched against the
goods received note and the incoming quality control release.

An invoice for a lot subsequently rejected at incoming control is credited in full. The
supplier disputes this treatment and the review should settle it: the agreement is
silent on whether the credit or the replacement is the primary remedy.

## Change control

Any change to cell chemistry, to the production line, or to the supplier's own
sub-suppliers requires Velora's prior written consent and a requalification of three
lots.

The secondary-sourcing consent that preceded the August 2025 batch was granted under
this clause. The clause worked; the qualification depth did not.

## Confidentiality

Both parties treat the commercial terms as confidential for the term of the agreement
and for 3 years after. Unit prices in particular are not to be disclosed to any third
party, which includes disclosure inside Velora beyond procurement, commercial and
finance.

## Audit

Velora may audit the supplier's production and quality records once per contract year on
20 working days notice, and additionally following any non-conformity, on 5 working days
notice.

Velora exercised the post-non-conformity right in September 2025. The audit report is
held by quality and is the basis for the dual-sourcing recommendation.

## Force majeure

Neither party is liable for delay caused by an event outside its reasonable control.
Port closure due to weather is expressly included, which is why the two late Q3 lots
carried no remedy.

The review should consider whether "expressly included" is the right treatment for an
event that recurs annually and is therefore foreseeable.

# Internal Pricing Memo — 2026 Model Year

Circulation: commercial team, support leads. Internal only.

## Recommended retail prices, 2026

| Model | 2025 RRP (EUR) | 2026 RRP (EUR) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Urban S | 2 090 | 2 190 | +4.8 % |
| Trail X | 3 190 | 3 290 | +3.1 % |
| Cargo Pro | 4 390 | 4 590 | +4.6 % |
| Aero helmet | 129 | 129 | — |
| Replacement 720 Wh pack | 690 | 740 | +7.2 % |
| Replacement 500 Wh pack | 520 | 550 | +5.8 % |

## Rationale

The increase on complete bicycles tracks the frame and cell unit-price movements agreed
in Q4 and is below the segment average of roughly 6 %. Holding the helmet at 129 EUR is
deliberate: it is the entry point to the accessory range and its margin is not the
reason it exists in the catalogue.

The replacement pack increase is larger than the bicycle increase because the pack price
carries the full cell cost without the volume dilution of a complete build. Support
should expect complaints on this line specifically, since a customer whose pack has left
its cell coverage period sees the replacement price directly.

## Gross margin by line

| Line | Gross margin |
|---|---|
| Complete bicycles | 34 % |
| Replacement packs | 22 % |
| Accessories | 48 % |
| Spare parts | 41 % |

These figures are internal and are not to be quoted to customers or to partners under
any circumstances.

## Discount authority

| Role | Maximum discount |
|---|---|
| Support agent | 5 % |
| Support lead | 10 % |
| Commercial manager | 20 % |
| Director | above 20 % |

A discount above the agent's authority requires the approving role's name on the order
record. Discounts are not applied to the replacement pack line without a commercial
manager's approval, because the 22 % margin does not absorb them.

## Competitive position

At EUR 3 290 the Trail X sits in the middle of the direct-to-consumer premium segment,
roughly EUR 200 below the nearest comparable specification from the two brands the
commercial team tracks and roughly EUR 400 above the volume brands.

The Urban S at EUR 2 190 is the most price-exposed line in the range: the city segment
has the most entrants and the least specification differentiation, and a EUR 100 move
by a competitor is visible in our conversion within a fortnight.

The Cargo Pro at EUR 4 590 faces two specialist competitors and very little from the
volume brands. Price elasticity on this line is low; availability matters more.

## Carriage and the free-delivery threshold

The EUR 1 500 free-delivery threshold on EU destinations means every complete bicycle
ships free and every accessory order below EUR 1 500 does not. Carriage on a bicycle
costs Velora roughly EUR 31 on the core lanes and more elsewhere, and that cost sits
inside the 34 % gross margin above.

The threshold is not a promotional lever. Lowering it moves accessory orders into free
carriage at a cost that the 48 % accessory margin does not absorb at low order values.

## VAT

All prices in this memo are inclusive of VAT at the rate of the destination country.
The margins stated are calculated on the VAT-exclusive value.

Support quoting a price to a customer quotes the inclusive price for their destination,
which the storefront calculates. A price quoted from this memo to a customer in a
different VAT jurisdiction will be wrong.

## Price changes in year

Prices are set annually and are not changed in year except where a supply cost movement
exceeds 10 %, which requires director approval. A customer who purchased before a price
increase has no entitlement to anything, and a customer who purchased before a decrease
has no entitlement to the difference.

## Replacement pack pricing, in detail

The 720 Wh pack at EUR 740 and the 500 Wh pack at EUR 550 carry the 22 % margin stated
above. The margin is thin because the pack price is dominated by cell cost, which is
roughly EUR 385 at the 2026 unit price for a 720 Wh pack of 100 cells.

Support will meet customers who compare the replacement pack price to the price of a
complete bicycle and conclude that Velora is profiteering on packs. The arithmetic above
is the answer, and it is internal: the response to the customer is the price, not the
cost breakdown.

## Spare parts

Spare parts carry a 41 % margin and are priced from the supplier catalogue plus a
standard uplift. Parts pricing is maintained by the workshop team and is not in this
memo.

## What is not in this memo

Promotional calendars, partner pricing, and trade terms. Those are held by the
commercial team and are not circulated to support.

## Price positioning by channel

Velora sells direct only. There is no wholesale price list for the consumer range and no
authorised reseller network for it, which means the recommended retail price is the
price and the concept of a street price does not apply.

Trade pricing exists for registered bicycle retailers on spare parts and on accessories
only, at a 30 % discount from the prices above, handled by the commercial team.

## Sensitivity

| Line | Volume sensitivity to a 5 % price rise |
|---|---|
| Urban S | High: −8 to −12 % volume |
| Trail X | Moderate: −4 to −6 % |
| Cargo Pro | Low: −1 to −3 % |
| Accessories | Low |
| Replacement packs | Very low: no alternative supplier |

The replacement pack row is the one to read carefully. Low sensitivity is not a licence
to price freely: a customer with no alternative who feels exploited is a customer who
does not buy the next bicycle, and the pack purchase typically sits at year three, right
before the replacement decision.

## Currency

Prices are set in EUR and converted to GBP at a rate reviewed monthly with a 3 % buffer.
A GBP price is not recalculated between reviews, so the effective GBP margin moves with
the rate inside the month.

USD prices exist for a small number of accessory orders and are not a supported channel.

## What support may say about price

The price, the VAT-inclusive figure for the customer's destination, and the fact that
Velora does not price match retroactively or operate student pricing.

What support may never say: the margin on any line, the unit cost of any component, the
supplier of any component, or any figure from this memo other than a retail price.

## Approval trail

Every discount above the agent's authority carries the approving role's name on the
order record. A discount without a name on it is reversed at month end and the agent is
asked for the approval.

## Review calendar

Prices are reviewed in Q4 for the following calendar year, at the same time as the
supplier unit prices are agreed. A mid-year change requires director approval and a
supply cost movement above 10 %.

# FAQ — Battery and Charging

## How far will I actually get on a charge?

The claimed range is measured on a flat route at 20 °C with a 75 kg rider in eco mode.
Real-world range on hilly terrain with a heavier load in boost mode is commonly 45 to
60 % of the claimed figure. This is not a defect and it is the same for every
manufacturer publishing a claimed range.

## Can I leave it on charge overnight?

Yes. The charger stops at 100 % and does not trickle. Leaving it plugged in does not
add cycles. However, storing the pack at 100 % for weeks does accelerate capacity
decline; if the bicycle is unused for more than a month, store the pack between 30 %
and 60 %.

## Does a partial charge count as a cycle?

No. A cycle is the cumulative equivalent of one full discharge and recharge. Charging
from 60 % to 100 % four times counts as roughly 1.6 cycles, not 4.

## Can I use a faster charger?

Only Velora-approved chargers. A non-approved charger that delivers more current than
the pack's specification degrades the cells, and that degradation is excluded from
coverage.

## What happens if it gets wet?

Riding in rain is fine; the pack is sealed to IP65. Immersion and pressure washing are
not. Water forced past the seals causes corrosion that appears weeks later as capacity
loss, and water damage is excluded.

## My capacity has dropped. Is that covered?

Coverage applies below 70 % of nominal capacity, inside the coverage period. A pack at
80 % after two years is behaving as specified. A pack at 65 % at month 14 with fewer
than 500 cycles is a claim.

## How do I know my cycle count?

Any Velora service partner reads it from the battery management system in under a
minute. There is currently no way to read it from the app.

## Where is my order?

Order references have the form `VLR-YYYY-NNNNN`. The tracking link is in the dispatch
email, which is sent when the item leaves the warehouse, not when the order is placed.

## Can I change the delivery address after ordering?

Before dispatch, yes, through support. After dispatch, the carrier can redirect within
the same country for a fee, and cannot redirect across a border.

## How long do I have to change my mind?

Thirty days on a complete bicycle, from the day it is delivered. Fourteen days on
accessories in the EU, seven in the UK. Custom builds and opened consumables are
treated differently — see the returns policy for the exact table.

## Do I pay to send it back?

Not on a bicycle inside the window. You do on accessories and on custom builds.

## My order arrived in two parcels on different days.

That is expected when an order contains a bicycle and accessories: they ship on
different services. Each part's return window runs from its own delivery date.

## Can I return a bicycle I have ridden?

Yes, within the window and in resaleable condition. Road dust and light tyre wear are
fine. Damage, missing parts and modifications are not.

## When do I get my money back?

Within 14 days of the return arriving at the warehouse, to the original payment method.

## Is the price I paid protected if it drops?

Velora does not operate a price-protection scheme. A price change after purchase is not
a refundable difference.

## Why does my range vary so much between rides?

Range depends on terrain, rider and cargo weight, assistance level, tyre pressure,
headwind and ambient temperature. Each of those moves range by 10 to 30 %, and together
they move it by more than a factor of two on an identical pack.

Cold weather is the single largest factor. A pack at 0 °C delivers roughly 70 % of the
range it delivers at 20 °C, and it recovers completely as it warms. This is chemistry,
not a defect, and it is not a covered failure.

## The range estimate on the display keeps changing.

It is calculated from the last 10 km of consumption. Start a hilly ride and it is
optimistic; it settles within a few kilometres. It is an estimate and it is described as
one, and its movement is not a defect.

## Can I buy a bigger battery?

No. Pack capacity is fixed by the frame bay and by the controller configuration. The
Cargo Pro accepts a second pack; the Trail X and Urban S do not.

## What happens to an old pack?

Return it to a Velora service partner or to any retailer selling batteries. Lithium-ion
packs must not go into household waste anywhere in the European Union. Velora takes
back packs at no charge, including packs not bought from Velora.

## Can I fly with the battery?

No. Packs above 100 Wh are not accepted in cabin or hold baggage by any commercial
airline operating under IATA rules, and every Velora pack is far above that. A bicycle
travelling by air travels without its pack.

## Can I take it on a train or a ferry?

Rules vary by operator. Most European rail operators accept an e-bike with the pack
fitted; several ferry operators require the pack to be removed and carried separately.
Check the operator.

## Does fast charging damage the pack?

Velora does not supply a fast charger. The supplied 4 A charger reaches 80 % in 2 h 30
on a 720 Wh pack and 2 h 00 on a 500 Wh pack. A higher-current charger from another
manufacturer will charge faster and will degrade the cells, and the resulting capacity
loss is excluded from coverage.

## My charger is warm. Is that normal?

Yes. The charger dissipates heat while charging and is warm to the touch throughout. A
charger that is too hot to hold, that smells, or that has a discoloured case should stop
being used and support contacted.

## How long does a pack last?

A pack that reaches 500 cycles at the capacity Velora specifies is at or above 70 % of
nominal. Beyond that it continues to work with progressively less range. Most riders
replace a pack when the reduced range starts constraining the rides they want to do,
which for a commuter is typically somewhere between year three and year five.

## Can Velora repair a pack rather than replace it?

Cell-level repair is offered on packs outside their coverage period where the failure is
confined to one cell group. Inside the coverage period Velora replaces rather than
repairs, because a repaired pack cannot be warranted to the same standard.

## What is the warranty on a replacement pack?

A pack supplied under an accepted claim carries the remainder of the original coverage
period, not a fresh one. A pack bought at the replacement price carries a new coverage
period of its own from its delivery date.

Customers regularly assume the opposite in both directions.

## Can I charge it at work and at home?

Yes. Where you charge makes no difference. How often you charge to full does: a pack
kept between 20 % and 80 % ages more slowly than one cycled from empty to full, though
the difference over a 500-cycle life is modest and not worth organising your life
around.

## Is it safe to charge indoors?

Yes, with the supplied charger, on a hard surface, not covered, and not blocking an exit.
Charge in a room with a smoke alarm. This is standard advice for any lithium-ion device
of this size and is not specific to Velora.

## What if the pack is damaged?

Stop using it. Do not charge it. A pack that has been dropped hard, crushed, punctured
or that has swollen must be assessed by a service partner, and Velora arranges collection
under dangerous-goods conditions at its own cost.

Do not put a damaged pack in any waste stream.

## How do I know if my pack is on the 2025 affected list?

Read the serial from the label on the underside of the pack or from the app under
Settings, Battery, Details, and give it to support. The check is exact and takes a
moment.

## Why does support keep asking for a cycle count I cannot read?

Because the coverage period is defined by it and the app does not show it. Any service
partner reads it in under a minute. Where the pack is on the affected list, the cycle
count is not needed at all.

## Does the guarantee start from when I ordered or when it arrived?

From delivery under the 2025 terms. Under the superseded terms it ran from purchase,
with delivery applying where the two differed by more than 30 days. Which applies to you
depends on your delivery date.

## Can I get the terms in writing?

Yes. The applicable document is sent on request and is the one in force at the date of
your delivery.

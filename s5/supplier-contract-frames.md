# Supplier Agreement — Frame Supply

Counterparty: Taleo Composites Sp. z o.o., Poznań. Effective 1 April 2025. Confidential.

## Scope

Supply of hydroformed aluminium frame sets for the Trail X, Urban S and Cargo Pro
ranges.

## Lead time and service level

The supplier commits to a delivery service level of **60 days** from purchase order to
delivery at the Velora assembly site.

Tooling changes for a new model year require an additional 90 days notice before the
first order under the new geometry.

## Incoming quality control

Velora applies an incoming quality control period of **5 working days** from receipt
before frames are released to assembly. Frames are inspected at 5 % for weld integrity,
alignment within 1.5 mm, and paint adhesion.

## Warranty passed through

The supplier warrants frames against manufacturing defect for 60 months from delivery
to Velora. Velora's own frame warranty to the end customer is 60 months from delivery
to the customer, which means the pass-through is imperfect at the tail: a frame held in
stock for 6 months carries 54 months of supplier cover against 60 months of customer
cover, and the difference is Velora's exposure.

## Pricing

Frame set pricing is EUR 148 for Urban S, EUR 186 for Trail X, and EUR 240 for Cargo
Pro at committed annual volumes. Below-commitment volumes attract a 12 % surcharge.

## Termination

Either party may terminate on 180 days written notice.

## Volume commitment

Velora commits to a minimum annual offtake of 18 000 frame sets across the three
ranges. The commitment is measured on frames delivered and accepted.

Below-commitment volumes attract a 12 % surcharge on the unit prices stated above,
applied retrospectively at year end.

## Tooling

Tooling for each frame geometry is owned by Velora and held at the supplier's site. The
supplier maintains it and Velora pays for refurbishment at the intervals set out in
Schedule 3.

A change of geometry requires 90 days notice before the first order under the new
tooling, plus a first-article inspection at Velora's site before series production.

## First-article inspection

Every new geometry, and every geometry after a tooling refurbishment, requires a
first-article inspection of three sets: dimensional check against drawing, weld
sectioning on one set, and paint adhesion testing.

Series production does not begin until the first-article report is signed by Velora
quality. The report typically takes 10 working days from receipt of the samples, which
sits outside the 60-day delivery service level.

## Finish

Frames are supplied painted and cleared to the colour references in Schedule 4. Colour
tolerance is ΔE ≤ 1.5 against the reference panel. Frames outside tolerance are
non-conforming.

Paint defects found after assembly — which happens, because assembly handles the frame
more than incoming inspection does — are credited at frame value where the defect is
demonstrably a supply defect and not handling damage.

## Alignment

Rear triangle alignment within 1.5 mm and head tube alignment within 0.5 mm. Alignment
outside tolerance is the most common non-conformity and is checked on 5 % of every lot.

An out-of-tolerance frame that reaches assembly is usually discovered when the wheel
does not centre, at which point Velora has invested the assembly labour. The 5 %
sampling rate is a commercial compromise and the review should revisit it.

## Packaging

Frames are supplied individually bagged with the head tube and dropouts protected, 12
sets to a pallet. Damage attributable to inadequate packaging is a supplier cost.

## Liability

The supplier's liability is limited to replacement value plus direct handling costs.
Consequential loss is excluded, on the same basis as the cell agreement.

## Governing law

Polish law. Disputes are referred to the courts of Poznań, proceedings in English.

## Delivery terms and Incoterms

DAP Velora assembly site, 12 sets per pallet, deliveries booked 2 working days ahead.

## Payment terms

60 days from invoice date, matched against the goods received note and the incoming
quality control release.

## Change control

Any change to alloy, to the welding process, or to the paint system requires Velora's
prior written consent and a first-article inspection.

## Confidentiality

Commercial terms confidential for the term and for 3 years after. Frame set unit prices
are not disclosed outside procurement, commercial and finance.

## Audit

Once per contract year on 20 working days notice, and following any non-conformity on
5 working days notice.

## Capacity and allocation

The supplier commits to reserve capacity for Velora's firm forecast window. Where the
supplier's total order book exceeds its capacity, Velora's firm-window orders take
priority over the supplier's spot business.

This clause has not been tested. Procurement's view is that it would be difficult to
enforce and that the practical protection is the supplier's consistent performance
rather than the wording.

## Sustainability reporting

The supplier reports annually on energy source mix and on aluminium recycled content.
The 2025 report gives 62 % recycled content across the three geometries.

Velora does not currently publish these figures and has no environmental reporting
obligation that requires them. They are collected because a customer-facing claim would
need them and because procurement expects the obligation to arrive.

## Force majeure

Standard. Port and border closure included.
